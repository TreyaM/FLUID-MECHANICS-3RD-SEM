# FLUID-MECHANICS-3RD-SEM
Experimental Investigation of Low Cost Jet Spray Breakup for Aero-Propulsive Applications

# Summary
# Work:
Obtained the breakup and penetration length, density and intensity profile and the Sauter mean diameter via MATLAB analysis by varying the mass flow rate of water through a BOSCH AQT-3512 jet.
# Results:
Conducted an experimental jet atomization study (Bosch AQT-3512 pressure washer, 3/4/5 mm nozzle diameters, gravimetric mass flow measurement, MATLAB-based image processing for droplet sizing) to characterize primary and secondary breakup regimes and spray morphology as functions of nozzle geometry.
Measured breakup length increasing from 180.19 mm to 308.92 mm across the 3–5 mm nozzle range, and a penetration length peaking at 512.50 mm for the 4 mm nozzle, attributed the trend to competing effects of jet inertial mass (resistance to Kelvin-Helmholtz surface instability) and drag-driven breakup regime transition at larger nozzle widths.
Quantified droplet size distribution for the 5 mm nozzle via Sauter Mean Diameter (D32 = 2071.40 μm; D10 = 1808.27 μm, D30 = 1936.20 μm).
